# Envy Is A Really Good Drug

Been a while since I've been here. I'm not going to dwell on that right now, because I feel pretty shitty.

---

Anyway, I saw some guy's OffSec portfolio yesterday and it made me feel really shitty.

It looked like fucking gold. His LinkedIn too. He even had certs and actual quantifiable stuff.

Am I a fucking LARPer?

Have I just been LARPing as a red teamer all this while?

What the fuck am I doing with my life?

---

Anyway, that's why I feel shitty, and why I'm very envious.

As such, I plan to start documenting a whole lot more, because bro documents a lot.

I'm kinda feeling like Patrick Bateman right now, as if I just had a long look at Paul Allen's business card and now my whole reality is crumbling.

I hate this.
